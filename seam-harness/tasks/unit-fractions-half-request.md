Determine all triples of positive integers \(x \le y \le z\) satisfying

\[
\frac1x+\frac1y+\frac1z=\frac12.
\]

Give the complete list and a rigorous proof that no solutions are missing. Use
exact arithmetic. The final response should be a self-contained mathematical
argument rather than a report about agents or a brute-force program. It may use
bounds, factorization, divisor enumeration, or other elementary number-theoretic
methods, and it should verify the listed triples directly.
