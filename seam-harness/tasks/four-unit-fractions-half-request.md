Determine the exact number of quadruples of positive integers

\[
x \le y \le z \le w
\]

satisfying

\[
\frac1x+\frac1y+\frac1z+\frac1w=\frac12.
\]

Give a self-contained rigorous proof that the count is complete. Include an
auditable table giving the number of solutions for every feasible pair
\((x,y)\), together with row totals for each \(x\) and the grand total. Derive
finite bounds and use exact arithmetic throughout. A factor-pair or divisor
enumeration lemma for the final two denominators is welcome. The final answer
need not print every quadruple, but its table and method must be detailed enough
for a reader to reproduce the count without trusting a brute-force program.
